### 🎓 Master's thesis — Casa Paganini–InfoMus

Validating kinematic measures of movement smoothness for **DanzArTe**, an art-based movement-therapy system used with older adults in collaboration with the geriatric departments of E.O. Galliera Hospital, Genoa.

I built a reproducible Python pipeline over Qualisys optical motion-capture recordings and tested three smoothness metrics — Jerk RMS, LDLJ and SPARC — against two independent references: expert annotation, and labels fixed by experimental protocol. The two references disagreed, and that turned out to be the most interesting result in the work.

I wrote the metrics from their published definitions rather than using the laboratory's existing library, so that every number stayed traceable to a formula someone could check. When I compared the two, they disagreed strongly — and I reported it back to the library's developers.

`Python` &nbsp;`NumPy` &nbsp;`pandas` &nbsp;`SciPy` &nbsp;`scikit-learn` &nbsp;`Matplotlib`
